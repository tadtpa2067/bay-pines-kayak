Booking Policies & Weather Guide

Review our terms regarding customer cancellations, weather rules, and tidal safety safeguards before booking your watercraft.

📅 Customer Cancellation Policy

  • 24+ Hours Advance Notice: Eligible for a 100% full refund returned to the card on file, or a 1-year flexible reservation voucher credit.
  • Inside the 24-Hour Window: All bookings inside this threshold are completely non-refundable and cannot be split or altered.
  • Late Arrival / No-Show: Deliveries are timed precisely around high tides. If you are more than 20 minutes late without text notice, the slot is cleared without a refund.

⚡ Weather & Low-Tide Safety Rules

Safety is our top priority. We track live radar and local water level indicators. We will issue automatic closure notifications if environmental conditions present hazards.

  • Our Operational Triggers: High winds exceeding 15 knots, local lightning strikes within 10 miles, or low tides dropping below 0.9 ft.
  • Our Safety Guarantee: If our mobile operations team cancels your booking due to weather or unsafe low tides before launch, you receive a 100% full financial refund.
📌 Mobile Booking Note: All kayak checkouts must be confirmed online in advance. Because we deliver assets directly to the shoreline based strictly on reservations, on-site walk-ups cannot be accommodated at the trailer.
