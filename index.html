Frequently Asked Questions

Where exactly do we meet for launch and retrieval?

We operate directly out of War Veterans Memorial Park in Bay Pines, FL. Our mobile kayak trailer will meet you right at the designated waterfront launch area near the boat ramps. Look for our branded truck and trailer!

What is included with my kayak rental?

Every single and tandem rental includes high-quality lightweight paddles, a properly sized Coast Guard-approved Life Jacket (PFD), and a waterproof dry bag to keep your phone, keys, and wallet safe while on the water.

Do you offer discounts for veterans and military personnel?

Yes! As a veteran-owned business, we proudly offer a 10% discount for active-duty military, veterans, and local first responders. Be sure to select the military promotion tier during your online booking.

What happens if the weather in Pinellas County is bad?

Your safety is our top priority. If severe weather, high winds, or lightning strikes affect the Bay Pines area on your scheduled rental day, we will notify you to issue a full refund or help you reschedule to another clear day.
